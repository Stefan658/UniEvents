## Deep Research Workflow

Copy this checklist and track your progress:

```
Deep Research Progress:
- [ ] Step 1: Define research scope and objectives
- [ ] Step 2: Conduct initial exploratory research
- [ ] Step 3: Deep dive into key areas
- [ ] Step 4: Cross-reference and validate findings
- [ ] Step 5: Synthesize and analyze information
- [ ] Step 6: Generate structured research report
```

## **Step 1: Define research scope and objectives**
Clearly establish the research parameters: Main research question, scope boundaries, depth requirements, and expected deliverables.

## **Step 2: Conduct initial exploratory research**
Begin with broad reconnaissance to map the landscape:
- Use `google_web_search` to find general information, definitions, and context.
- Identify key authoritative sources and recent publications.
- Assess information gaps to note what needs deeper investigation.

## **Step 3: Deep dive into key areas**
For each identified key area, conduct a detailed investigation:
- Formulate targeted queries using `google_web_search` for specific subtopics.
- Use `web_fetch` to extract and read the complete text content of high-quality web sources, data, or technical specifications.

## **Step 4: Cross-reference and validate findings**
Ensure accuracy and reliability:
- Cross-check important information across multiple sources using `google_web_search`.
- Identify contradictions and check the recency of the data.
- Flag uncertainties where information is unclear or disputed.

## **Step 5: Synthesize and analyze information**
Transform collected data into insights: Look for patterns, link concepts across sources, and build a coherent narrative that directly answers the research question with evidence.

## **Step 6: Generate structured research report**
Create a comprehensive, well-organized final report using the following structure:
1. **Executive Summary**: Brief overview and 3-5 main points.
2. **Introduction**: Research scope and methodology.
3. **Main Body**: Detailed findings organized by themes.
4. **Analysis & Synthesis**: Insights and implications.
5. **Conclusions**: Direct answers to research questions.
6. **Sources & References**: List of URLs and sources consulted.

## Best Practices
- **Iterative approach**: Refine research questions as understanding deepens.
- **Primary tools**: Rely heavily on `google_web_search` and `web_fetch` to gather real-time data.
- Prefer official/primary sources when available.
- Always record limitations and uncertainty, especially when data is incomplete, dynamic, or authentication-protected.
